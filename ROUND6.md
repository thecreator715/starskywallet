# Starsky round 6

All of this is in the wallet now (index.html rebuilt). Everything is tested; the limits are stated per section.

## Convert all to LUMA (Portfolio page)
Card under your tokens. Live quotes from the pools and order books, a total, per-token checkboxes, a price-impact limit (default 1%), optional removal of emptied token lines, one confirmation, then one swap per token in order. A failed swap is explained and the rest continue. Large totals need typing CONVERT. Pool shares and tokens with no route are greyed out with the reason. NFTs are never converted.

## NFT toolkit — 50 ideas (src/live/nftplus.ts, 30 tests)
Rarity rank, trait counts and filters, sweep the floor, floor/price series/change, similar items, saved searches, compare, leaderboard, suggested price, expiry, bundles, Dutch and English auctions, offers inbox with counters, collection offers, auto-lower with a hard minimum, batch CSV listing, proceeds breakdown, royalty rules and earnings, mint phases and countdown, lazy-mint plan, seeded reveal, trait generator, per-wallet mint caps, verification, airdrop dry run, portfolio value and cost basis, spam score, tags, wishlist alerts, transfer safety check, bulk-burn guard, metadata health, provenance, impersonation check, image fingerprint (average hash), flagged collections, plain-words transaction preview, wash-trading flags, share text, embed snippet, activity feed, NFT-gated perks, bridge status labels.
On screen: the **NFT tools** card on the NFTs and Marketplace pages (sale proceeds, suggested price, auto-lower, Dutch price, loan limit, batch check).
Honest limits: floor history, rarity over a whole collection and image fingerprints need the indexer, which does not cover NFTs yet, so they work only from NFTs and sales the wallet has seen. Auctions, auto-lower rules and scheduled listings cannot run while the wallet is closed; they need on-chain offers or the wallet open. On the NFT pages themselves: My NFTs shows a rarity badge, a possible-spam badge with a hide switch, tags with a tag filter, and an estimated worth at each collection's lowest listing; an NFT's page shows rarity rank, collection floor, similar items, history, warnings (look-alike collection names, wash trading, metadata problems) and a copy-share-text button. Not wired yet: auctions, offers inbox, mint phases, airdrops and the other creator tools have logic and tests but no page.

## NFT-backed loans (src/live/nftloan.ts, 7 tests)
Rules engine: loan to value (limit 50%), valuation = the lowest of floor, last sale and your estimate, simple interest, repayment amount, states (offered, funded, overdue, claimable), what each side can do, cushion and risk, plain-words terms. **Not live:** the escrow step. `nftescrow.ts` (8 tests) builds the exact transactions: a per-loan escrow account whose own key is disabled and which is controlled by borrower + lender + a neutral operator with 2 of 3 signing, the NFT lock offer, loan and repayment payments, release offers, who may co-sign a release and when, a checker that confirms an escrow account is locked down, and a hash of the agreement. The XRP Ledger cannot enforce a deadline itself, so the operator's co-signature after the deadline is the one trusted step; that co-signing service and the Loans screen are not built, so nothing can be borrowed yet.
