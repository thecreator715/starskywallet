# Round 5: 50 additions (Starsky 17, Control panel 17, LUMA Scan 16)

## Starsky (Settings page "Wallet toolbox", plus Send page)
1. Max button on Send: everything spendable after reserve and fee
2. Fee now: current cost of a payment and how busy the network is
3. Notes on any transaction hash
4. Contacts: export and import as CSV (bad lines reported, duplicates skipped)
5. Encrypted settings backup and restore (never contains the vault or keys)
6. Where your money is: allocation bar by token
7. Reserve you can get back: remove empty token lines in one click
8. Dust finder: tiny balances listed
9. Payment reminders (weekly or monthly; never pay by themselves)
10. Large-payment guard: retype the last 4 characters of the recipient
11. 7- and 30-day digest: in, out, net, most active counterparties
12. Yearly payments CSV export
13. Wallet health score with a to-do list
14. Address checker: valid, wrong network, or garbage
15. Transaction explainer: any hash in plain words
16. Balance over time (daily snapshots, sparkline)
17. Guard threshold setting for 10

## Control panel (new tab "Ops Toolkit", Ctrl+K anywhere)
1. Search everything (incidents, runbooks, tasks, notes, chains)
2. Audit log viewer with filters and CSV export
3. "Who does what" summary per person
4. Timeline: incidents, maintenance, approvals, tasks, shifts, invoice due dates
5. Runbooks with tick-through runs recorded by person and time (3 ready-made)
6. On-call rota with handover times
7. Post-incident review drafts from real incidents
8. Error budget against your uptime target
9. Config snapshots and "what changed" diff (secrets masked)
10. Key and token rotation reminders
11. Fleet chain list as CSV
12. Private notes on anything
13. Task board with due dates and overdue state
14. Fleet map: every chain's nodes, bridges and standing at a glance
15. Receivables aging (current, 1-30, 31-60, 61+ days)
16. Billed vs collected by month
17. Payment-reminder drafts in three tones (copy into your own email; the panel never sends)

1. Network pressure (light / moderate / heavy)

3. Close-time histogram
4. Slow-ledger list
5. Transaction type mix with failure rates
6. Biggest payments (with CSV)
7. Busiest accounts
8. Smart jump: paste anything (address, hash, ledger number, currency)
9. Compare two accounts side by side
10. Account activity heat map (weekday by hour)
11. Node health score with its parts

Limits: Scan figures cover only what the browser saw while open. Starsky reminders, notes and history live in this browser only (use the encrypted backup). Billing and reminders in the panel are drafts and records, never payments or emails.
